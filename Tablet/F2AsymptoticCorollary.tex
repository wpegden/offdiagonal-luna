\begin{lemma}
Let $s=s(n)\to\infty$ and let $a=a(n)\geq0$ satisfy $a=o(s)$.  With
$k=s+a$, the exact sum in
\noderef{F2RankSequenceBound} satisfies
\[
 \sum_{t=1}^{s-1}\binom kt
  2^{(s-1)(t+k)-\binom{t+1}{2}}
 \leq 2^{\frac32s^2+as-\frac52s+o(s)}.
\]
Equivalently, there is an error function $e=o(s)$ for which the left side
is at most $2^{\frac32s^2+as-\frac52s+e}$ eventually.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $t=s-j$.  Then
$\binom{s+a}{t}=\binom{s+a}{j+a}$, and the standard estimate
$\binom uv\leq(eu/v)^v$ gives
\[
 \binom{s+a}{s-j}2^{(s-1)(s-j+s+a)-\binom{s-j+1}{2}}
 \leq
 \left(\frac{e(s+a)}{a+1}\right)^{j+a}
 2^{s^2/2+s(s+a)-3s/2-(s+a)+3j/2-j^2/2}.
\]
The exponent depending on $j$ is a concave quadratic.  Its maximum is at
$j=\log_2(e(s+a)/(a+1))+3/2$, up to an additive constant, so uniformly in
$t$ each summand is at most
\[
 \left(\frac{e(s+a)}{a+1}\right)^a
 2^{s^2/2+s(s+a)-3s/2-(s+a)+O((\log s)^2)}.
\]
Since $a=o(s)$, the first factor is $2^{o(s)}$, and the $s$ summands absorb
the remaining $O(\log s)$ term into $2^{o(s)}$.  Substituting $k=s+a$
leaves the exponent
$\frac32s^2+as-\frac52s+o(s)$, as claimed.
\end{proof}
