\begin{lemma}
For integers $4\leq s\leq k$, let $D_2(s)$ be the specialized old pair
digraph from \noderef{F2PairDigraph}.  It is loopless and $T_s$-free, has
\[
 N=2^{2s-3}-2^{s-1}-2^{s-2}+1
\]
vertices, and satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D_2(s))\leq
 \sum_{t=1}^{s-1}\binom kt
 2^{(s-1)(t+k)-\binom{t+1}{2}}.
\]
The sum intentionally counts ambient vector tuples, so it may overcount
projective points and nonzero representatives.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $p=s-1$ and use the construction of
\noderef{F2PairDigraph}, which is the old pair construction at
$G(s-2,2)$.  The loopless and triangular-freeness assertions and the exact
vertex count are supplied by \noderef{F2PairDigraphProperties}.

It remains to count tuples.  A forward-independent $k$-tuple in $D_2(s)$
gives an ambient tuple
$(\mathbf a_1,\mathbf b_1,\ldots,\mathbf a_k,\mathbf b_k)$ in
$(\mathbb F_2^p)^{2k}$ satisfying
\[
 \langle\mathbf a_i,\mathbf b_j\rangle=1\quad(i\leq j).
\]
We deliberately forget the nonzero and projective restrictions.  Thus the
number of desired tuples is at most the ambient number counted by
\noderef{F2RankSequenceBound}.

For completeness, that count uses the rank sequence
$r_0=0$ and
$r_i=\dim\operatorname{span}(\mathbf a_1,\ldots,\mathbf a_i)$.  The
diagonal equation gives $r_1=1$, each increment is zero or one, and a final
rank $t$ has at most $\binom kt$ possible increment sets.  For a fixed
sequence, a rank increase contributes at most $2^p$ choices for
$\mathbf a_i$ and $2^{p-r_i}$ for $\mathbf b_i$; a non-increase contributes
at most $2^{r_i}$ and $2^{p-r_i}$.  Multiplication gives
$2^{pt+pk-\binom{t+1}{2}}$.  Summing over $1\leq t\leq p=s-1$ gives the
displayed inequality.
\end{proof}
