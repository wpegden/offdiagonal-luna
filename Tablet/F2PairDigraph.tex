\begin{definition}
For $s\geq4$, put $p=s-1$ and identify the points of
$G(s-2,2)$ with the nonzero vectors of $\mathbb F_2^p$.  Define
$D_2(s)$ to be the old pair digraph of
\[
 G(s-2,2):\quad x\sim y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \langle x,y\rangle=0.
\]
Thus the vertices of $D_2(s)$ are pairs $(x,y)$ with
$\langle x,y\rangle=1$, and
$(x,y)\to(x',y')$ exactly when $\langle x,y'\rangle=0$.
The ambient-vector description deliberately permits zero vectors when it is
used for an upper bound on tuple counts.
\end{definition}
