\begin{lemma}
For every $s\geq4$, the specialized digraph $D_2(s)$ of
\noderef{F2PairDigraph} is loopless and $T_s$-free, and its exact number of
vertices is
\[
 |V(D_2(s))|=(2^{s-1}-1)(2^{s-2}-1)
 =2^{2s-3}-2^{s-1}-2^{s-2}+1.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $p=s-1$.  A vertex is a pair of nonzero vectors $(x,y)$ with
$\langle x,y\rangle=1$.  An arc from a pair to itself would require the
same inner product to be zero, so the digraph is loopless.  The triangular
orthogonality obstruction used for the old pair construction says that
there are no $s$ pairs with diagonal inner products one and all strictly
forward inner products zero.  By
\noderef{OldPairDigraphProperties}, this makes $D_2(s)$ $T_s$-free.

For the count, there are $2^p-1$ choices for $x$.  The nonzero solutions of
$\langle x,y\rangle=1$ form an affine hyperplane of size $2^{p-1}$, so after
the projective identification used in the paper the ordered nonedge-pair
count is
$(2^p-1)(2^{p-1}-1)$.  Expanding this product gives the stated formula.
\end{proof}
