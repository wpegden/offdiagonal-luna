\begin{definition}
For a finite loopless digraph $D$, let
$\operatorname{fwi}_k(D)$ be the cardinality of the finite type of ordered
$k$-tuples satisfying the forward-independence condition of
\noderef{ForwardIndependentTuple}.  The cardinality counts tuples rather than
unordered sets and therefore retains repeated entries when they satisfy the
condition.
\end{definition}
