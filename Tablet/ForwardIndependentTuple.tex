\begin{definition}
Let $D$ be a loopless digraph.  An ordered $k$-tuple
$(v_1,\ldots,v_k)\in V(D)^k$ is forward independent when, for every pair of
indices $i<j$, the directed arc $(v_i,v_j)$ is absent.  Entries are not
required to be distinct; thus repeated entries are handled by the same
ordered-tuple condition, with looplessness ensuring that a repeated entry
does not create a forbidden self-arc.
\end{definition}
