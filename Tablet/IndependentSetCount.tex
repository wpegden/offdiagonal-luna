\begin{definition}
For a finite loop-aware graph $G$, $i_k(G)$ is the number of independent
vertex sets of cardinality $k$.  It is represented as the cardinality of the
finite type of finsets $S\subseteq V(G)$ with $|S|=k$ and with no adjacency
between distinct members.  Thus each set is counted once, rather than once
for each ordering of its vertices.
\end{definition}
