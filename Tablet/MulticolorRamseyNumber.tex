\begin{definition}
For positive integers $s$ and $\ell$, the multicolor Ramsey number
$r(s;\ell)$ is the least integer $n$ such that every $\ell$-coloring of the
edges of the complete graph on $n$ vertices contains a monochromatic clique of
size $s$.  The tablet represents colorings and monochromatic cliques by the
finite labelled structures of \noderef{CompleteColoring} and
\noderef{MonochromaticClique}, and takes the natural-number infimum of the
orders with this universal property.
\end{definition}
