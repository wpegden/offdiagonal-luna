\begin{lemma}
Let $G$ be a finite loop-aware undirected graph and write
$D=\operatorname{OldPairDigraph}(G)$.  Let
$t,q,k,n,h\in\mathbb N$ satisfy $2\leq t$ and
\[
 32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)\leq k.
\]
Suppose there is a function
$c:(\operatorname{Fin}(k)\to\{\mathsf{false},\mathsf{true}\})\to\mathbb N$
such that
\[
 \sum_{z:\operatorname{Fin}(k)\to\{\mathsf{false},\mathsf{true}\}}c(z)
 =\operatorname{fwi}_k(D),
\]
and, for every signature $z$,
\[
 c(z)\leq(n^2)^{\sum_{i:\operatorname{Fin}(k)}
   \begin{cases}1&\text{if }z(i)=\mathsf{true},\\0&\text{if }z(i)=\mathsf{false},\end{cases}}
 h^{k-\sum_{i:\operatorname{Fin}(k)}
   \begin{cases}1&\text{if }z(i)=\mathsf{true},\\0&\text{if }z(i)=\mathsf{false},\end{cases}}
\]
and
\[
 c(z)>0\Longrightarrow
 \sum_{i:\operatorname{Fin}(k)}
   \begin{cases}1&\text{if }z(i)=\mathsf{true},\\0&\text{if }z(i)=\mathsf{false}\end{cases}
 \leq 32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q).
\]
If $h\leq n^2$, then
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D)\leq
 2^k(n^2)^{32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)}
 h^{k-32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a signature $z$, let $j(z)$ denote the displayed sum of its true
coordinates.  The assumed per-signature inequality gives
\[
 c(z)\leq(n^2)^{j(z)}h^{k-j(z)}.
\]
If $c(z)>0$, the second assumption gives
$j(z)\leq32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)$; if $c(z)=0$, its
contribution to the sum is zero.  Since $h\leq n^2$, increasing the
exponent of $n^2$ and decreasing the exponent of $h$ preserves or
increases the product, so every nonzero summand is at most
\[
 (n^2)^{32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)}
 h^{k-32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)}.
\]
There are exactly $2^k$ functions from $\operatorname{Fin}(k)$ to
$\{\mathsf{false},\mathsf{true}\}$.  Summing the per-signature bound and
using the assumed identity for the sum of the $c(z)$ proves the claimed
inequality.
\end{proof}
