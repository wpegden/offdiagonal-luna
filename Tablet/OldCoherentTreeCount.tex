\begin{lemma}
Let $K$ be a finite field with $q=|K|$ a prime power satisfying $q\geq16$,
and let $t\geq2$.  Put
$G=G_K(t)=\operatorname{PolarityGraph}(K,t)$ and
$D=\operatorname{OldPairDigraph}(G)$, using
\noderef{PolarityGraph} and \noderef{OldPairDigraph}.  Let
$n,d\in\mathbb N$ and $\lambda\in\mathbb R$ satisfy the parameter package
provided by \noderef{OldPolarityParameters}:
\[
 |V(G)|=n=\frac{q^{t+1}-1}{q-1},\qquad
 d=\frac{q^t-1}{q-1},
\]
$G$ is $d$-regular, its nonprincipal spectral parameter is
\[
 \lambda=\sqrt{d-\frac{q^{t-1}-1}{q-1}},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{NonprincipalSpectralBound}(G,\lambda)
 \text{ in the sense of \noderef{NonprincipalSpectralBound}},
\]
and
\[
 q^t/2\leq n\leq2q^t,\qquad
 q^{t-1}/2\leq d\leq2q^{t-1},\qquad
 \lambda\leq2\sqrt d.
\]
Assume also the expander-mixing inequality, in the ordered-edge convention,
for all vertex subsets $A,B$:
\[
 \left|e_G(A,B)-\frac d n|A||B|\right|
 \leq \lambda\sqrt{|A||B|}.
\]
Finally let $k\in\mathbb N$ satisfy $1\leq n$, $1\leq d$, and
\[
 w:=32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)\leq k.
\]
Then
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D)\leq
 8^k\left(\frac{\lambda^2}{d^2}\right)^{k-w}n^{2k}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $w=32tq\,\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)$.  By
\noderef{OldPolarityParameters}, the displayed values of $n,d,\lambda$ and
the spectral bound belong to this particular projective polarity graph; the
same package gives the displayed size and degree estimates.  The explicit
mixing assumption is the ordered-subset input required by
\noderef{AlonRodlBound}; it is stated explicitly here so that no unproved
spectral-to-mixing implication is hidden in this counting node.

A forward-independent $m$-tuple in $D$ is, by the vertex and arc
definitions in \noderef{OldPairDigraph}, exactly a coherent tuple
\[
 (a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_m,b_m)\in V(G)^{2m}
\]
with $a_i b_i\notin E(G)$ for every $i$ and
$a_i b_j\notin E(G)$ whenever $i<j$.  Form the rooted tree $\mathcal T$
whose root is the empty tuple and whose children are obtained by appending
one coherent pair.  Its vertices at level $k$ are therefore counted by
\operatorname{fwi}_k(D), using the definition of
\noderef{ForwardIndependentCount}.

Fix a coherent partial tuple
$\sigma=(a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_m,b_m)$ and put
\[
 B^\sigma=V(G)\setminus\bigcup_{i=1}^mN_G(a_i),\qquad
 A^\sigma=\left\{a\in V(G):
 |N_G(a)\cap B^\sigma|\leq\frac{d}{2n}|B^\sigma|\right\}.
\]
Apply the product estimate of \noderef{AlonRodlBound} to these two sets,
using the mixing inequality and $n,d>0$.  It gives
\[
 |A^\sigma||B^\sigma|\leq
 h:=\frac{4\lambda^2n^2}{d^2}.
\]
Any coherent child appending $(a,b)$ has $b\in B^\sigma$, because every
earlier $a_i b$ is a nonedge.  Mark the children with
$(a,b)\in A^\sigma\times B^\sigma$.  There are at most $h$ marked
children, while there are at most
\[
 \Delta=n^2
\]
children in total.  In the natural-valued signature encoding used by
\noderef{RootedTreeCounting}, integer floors of $h$ give the same inequality;
monotonicity of the powers allows the resulting estimate to be written with
the real number $h$ above.

It remains to bound unmarked runs.  If an appended pair is unmarked, then
$a\notin A^\sigma$, so more than a $d/(2n)$ fraction of $B^\sigma$ is
removed when passing to the next $B$-set.  Hence each such step multiplies
$|B^\sigma|$ by at most $1-d/(2n)$.  If a root-to-vertex path had more than
$w$ unmarked steps, then, since $w-1\geq16tq\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)$,
\[
 |B^\sigma|\leq n\left(1-\frac d{2n}\right)^{w-1}
 \leq2q^t\exp\left(-\frac d{2n}\,16tq\operatorname{Nat.log}(2,q)\right)<1.
\]
Here $d/n\geq1/(4q)$ follows from the displayed size and degree bounds, and
the final strict inequality follows from $q\geq16$ (changing between fixed
logarithm bases only strengthens the absolute constant).  But the last
second-coordinate $b_m$ belongs to $B^\sigma$ by coherence, a contradiction.
Thus every path has at most $w$ unmarked vertices.

We may now apply the marked-signature estimate
\noderef{RootedTreeCounting} with $\Delta=n^2$ and
$h=4\lambda^2n^2/d^2$.  The parameter package gives
$h\leq n^2$: indeed $d\geq q\geq16$ from its exact formula and
$\lambda\leq2\sqrt d$.  Since $k\geq w$, it follows that
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D)
 \leq 2^k(n^2)^w
 \left(\frac{4\lambda^2n^2}{d^2}\right)^{k-w}
 =2^k4^{k-w}
 \left(\frac{\lambda^2}{d^2}\right)^{k-w}n^{2k}.
\]
Finally $2^k4^{k-w}\leq8^k$, giving the claimed bound.
\end{proof}
