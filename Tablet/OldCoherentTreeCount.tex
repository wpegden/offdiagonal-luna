\begin{lemma}
Let $G$ be the graph in the old polarity construction and let
$D=\mathcal D(G)$.  Fix $t,q,k,n,h$ with $t\geq2$ and
$32tq\log q\leq k$.  Suppose the coherent-pair tree is partitioned by
binary signatures $z\in\{0,1\}^k$, with $c(z)$ paths of signature $z$, so
that
\[
 c(z)\leq(n^2)^{|z|}h^{k-|z|},\qquad
 c(z)>0\Longrightarrow |z|\leq32tq\log q,
\]
and that the sum of the signature counts is
$\operatorname{fwi}_k(D)$.  If $h\leq n^2$, then
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D)\leq
 2^k(n^2)^{32tq\log q}
 h^{k-32tq\log q}.
\]
For the polarity graph, the marking argument supplies these hypotheses
with $n=|V(G)|$ and $h$ bounded by $4\lambda^2n^2/d^2$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first displayed inequality bounds the number of paths having a fixed
signature: an unmarked step has at most $n^2$ choices and a marked step at
most $h$ choices.  The second inequality says that every nonzero signature
has at most $32tq\log q$ unmarked steps.  Therefore the sum over all
signatures is bounded by
\[
 \sum_{|z|\leq32tq\log q}(n^2)^{|z|}h^{k-|z|}.
\]
There are at most $2^k$ signatures, and $h\leq n^2$, so every summand is
at most the term with exponent $32tq\log q$.  This proves the claimed
bound.  In the old polarity argument, the children marked by
$A^\sigma\times B^\sigma$ are bounded by
\[
 |A^\sigma||B^\sigma|\leq4\lambda^2n^2/d^2
\]
using \noderef{AlonRodlBound}; the coherent tuples are the tree paths by
\noderef{OldPairDigraph}.  Thus the abstract signature hypotheses are
exactly the interface delivered by the coherent-tree analysis, whose final
count is also the rooted-tree estimate of
\noderef{RootedTreeCounting}.
\end{proof}
