\begin{definition}
Let $G$ be a finite loop-aware undirected graph.  Its old pair digraph
$\mathcal D(G)$ has vertex set
\[
 \{(a,b)\in V(G)^2:ab\notin E(G)\}
\]
and has an arc $(a,b)\to(a',b')$ exactly when $ab'\in E(G)$.  The ordered
pair is part of the vertex, so $(a,b)$ and $(b,a)$ are generally different.
The construction is loopless: a loop at $(a,b)$ would simultaneously say
$ab\in E(G)$ and $ab\notin E(G)$.
\end{definition}
