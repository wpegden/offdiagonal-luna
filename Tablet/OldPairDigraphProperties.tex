\begin{lemma}
Let $G$ be a finite loop-aware graph with no vertices
$a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_s,b_s$ satisfying
\[
 a_i b_i\notin E(G)\quad(1\leq i\leq s),
 \qquad a_i b_j\in E(G)\quad(i<j).
\]
Then the old pair digraph $\mathcal D(G)$ of
\noderef{OldPairDigraph} is loopless and contains no copy of $T_s$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The loop assertion follows directly from the construction: a pair $(a,b)$
is a vertex only when $ab$ is a nonedge, whereas a loop at $(a,b)$ would
require $ab$ to be an edge.  For the tournament assertion, suppose that
$(a_i,b_i)$, $1\leq i\leq s$, form a transitive tournament in their displayed
order.  Vertex membership gives $a_i b_i\notin E(G)$, and the arc condition
in $\noderef{OldPairDigraph}$ gives $a_i b_j\in E(G)$ whenever $i<j$.
These are exactly the forbidden two-row triangular incidences, a
contradiction.  Thus both conclusions hold.
\end{proof}
