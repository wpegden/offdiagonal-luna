\begin{lemma}
For every $\delta>0$ there is an integer $L>0$ such that whenever
$s\geq L$ and $k\geq Ls$, one can choose a power of two $q$ satisfying
\[
 q\leq\frac{\delta}{200}\frac{k/s}{\log(k/s)}\leq2q,
\]
and the old polarity-pair digraph $D=D(s-2,q)$ is $T_s$-free,
\[
 |V(D)|\geq\frac{q^{2(s-2)}}2,
\]
and
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D)\leq
 \left(32q^{,2(s-2)-(s-3)(1-\delta/5)}\right)^k.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the least power of two in the interval specified in the statement;
for $L$ large the interval contains such a power and $q$ is large.  Put
$t=s-2$ and take the polarity graph $G(t,q)$, whose vertices are the
one-dimensional subspaces of $\mathbb F_q^{t+1}$.  The pair digraph has
vertices $(a,b)$ with $a b$ a nonedge and an arc $(a,b)\to(a',b')$ exactly
when $a b'$ is an edge.  The triangular orthogonality obstruction makes it
$T_{t+2}=T_s$-free, and the exact point and degree counts give
$|V(D)|\geq q^{2t}/2$.

The forward-independent tuples form the rooted coherent-pair tree.  Set
$h=4\lambda^2n^2/d^2$ and $\Delta=n^2$, where $n,d,\lambda$ are the
polarity graph's order, degree, and second-eigenvalue parameters.  The
Alon--Roedl product estimate of \noderef{AlonRodlBound} marks at most $h$
children at each step.  Every
path has at most $w=32tq\log q$ unmarked steps, because each unmarked step
shrinks the remaining common-neighbour set by the factor
$1-d/(2n)$ and after $w$ steps that set would have cardinality less than one.
The rooted-tree estimate of \noderef{RootedTreeCounting} gives
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D)\leq
 8^k(\lambda^2/d^2)^{k-w}n^{2k}.
\]
The polarity estimates $n\asymp q^t$, $d\asymp q^{t-1}$, and
$\lambda\leq2\sqrt d$, together with the selected value of $q$ and the
large-$L$ inequalities, reduce this to the displayed bound.  All constants
are absorbed by the factor $32$ and by increasing $L$.
\end{proof}
