\begin{lemma}
For every $\delta>0$ there is an integer $L>0$ such that whenever
$s\geq L$ and $k\geq Ls$, one can choose a power of two $q$ satisfying
\[
 q\leq\frac{\delta}{200}\frac{k/s}{\log(k/s)}\leq2q.
\]
For the polarity graph $G=G(s-2,q)$, let
$D=\mathcal D(G)$ be the old pair digraph of
\noderef{OldPairDigraph}.  Then $D$ is $T_s$-free,
\[
 |V(D)|\geq\frac{q^{2(s-2)}}2,
\]
and
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D)\leq
 \left(32q^{\,2(s-2)-(s-3)(1-\delta/5)}\right)^k.
\]
The statement records the polarity graph's triangular obstruction as part
of the choice of $G$; this is the hypothesis used by
\noderef{OldPairDigraphProperties}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the least power of two in the indicated interval.  For $L$ large
the interval contains such a power and $q$ is large.  Put $t=s-2$ and take
the polarity graph $G(t,q)$ on the one-dimensional subspaces of
$\mathbb F_q^{t+1}$.  Its orthogonality relation has the triangular
obstruction: there are no pairs $(a_i,b_i)_{i=1}^{t+2}$ with
$a_i b_i\notin E(G)$ and $a_i b_j\in E(G)$ for every $i<j$.

Apply the construction of \noderef{OldPairDigraph} to this $G$.  The
obstruction and \noderef{OldPairDigraphProperties}, with $s=t+2$, give
looplessness and the absence of $T_{t+2}=T_s$.  The exact point and degree
counts recorded in \noderef{OldPolarityParameters} for the polarity graph give
$|V(D)|\geq q^{2t}/2$.

It remains to count forward-independent tuples.  By the definition in
\noderef{OldPairDigraph}, such a tuple is exactly a coherent tuple
\[
 (a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_k,b_k),\qquad
 a_i b_i\notin E(G),\quad a_i b_j\notin E(G)\ (i<j).
\]
The coherent tuples form the rooted tree obtained by appending one pair.
For a partial tuple $\sigma$, let
$B^\sigma=V(G)\setminus\bigcup_iN_G(a_i)$ and let
$A^\sigma$ consist of vertices having at most
$d|B^\sigma|/(2n)$ neighbours in $B^\sigma$.  The Alon--R\"odl estimate
\noderef{AlonRodlBound} gives
\[
 |A^\sigma||B^\sigma|\leq4\lambda^2n^2/d^2.
\]
Mark the extensions from $A^\sigma\times B^\sigma$.  Each tree vertex has
at most $\Delta=n^2$ children, and the displayed product bounds the marked
children by $h=4\lambda^2n^2/d^2$.  Every unmarked extension shrinks
$|B^\sigma|$ by at least the factor $1-d/(2n)$.  Since
$w=32tq\log q$, the polarity estimates $n\leq2q^t$ and
$d\geq q^{t-1}/2$ show that a root path has at most $w$ unmarked steps.

The coherent-tree estimate \noderef{OldCoherentTreeCount}, which is the
application of \noderef{RootedTreeCounting} to these marked extensions,
therefore yields
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_k(D)\leq
 8^k(\lambda^2/d^2)^{k-w}n^{2k}.
\]
Finally $\lambda\leq2\sqrt d$, the selected value of $q$, and
$w\leq\delta k/5$ imply, after increasing $L$ to absorb fixed factors,
\[
 8^k(\lambda^2/d^2)^{k-w}n^{2k}
 \leq\left(32q^{\,2t-(t-1)(1-\delta/5)}\right)^k.
\]
Substituting $t=s-2$ proves the lemma.
\end{proof}
