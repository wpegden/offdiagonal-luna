\begin{helper}
Let $B$ be a nondegenerate bilinear form on a finite-dimensional vector
space $V$ over a finite field $K$, and let $W$ be a subspace.  Then
\[
 \dim W^{\perp}=\dim V-\dim W.
\]
Moreover, if $|K|\geq2$, the projective points of the orthogonal complement
are bounded by
\[
 \left|\mathbb P(W^{\perp})\right|
 \leq 2|K|^{\dim W^{\perp}-1},
\]
with the zero-dimensional case interpreted in the usual empty-projective-
space sense.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Nondegeneracy gives the rank-nullity identity for the restriction map from
$V$ to the dual of $W$.  Its kernel is $W^{\perp}$, and hence its nullity is
$\dim V-\dim W$.  A vector space of dimension $r$ has
$(|K|^r-1)/(|K|-1)$ one-dimensional subspaces.  For $r=0$ this is zero.  For
$r\geq1$ and $|K|\geq2$, the numerator is at most $|K|^r$ and
$|K|-1\geq |K|/2$, so the quotient is at most
$2|K|^{r-1}$.  Applying this with $r=\dim W^{\perp}$ proves the second
inequality.
\end{proof}
