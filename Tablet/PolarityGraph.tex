\begin{definition}
Let $K$ be a finite field and let $t\geq2$.  The projective polarity graph
$G_K(t)$ has as its vertices the one-dimensional subspaces of
$K^{t+1}$, equivalently the projectivization of the vector space of maps
$\operatorname{Fin}(t+1)\to K$.  Two vertices, including a vertex with
itself, are adjacent exactly when representatives $x,y$ have dot product
\[
 \langle x,y\rangle=\sum_{i=0}^{t}x_i y_i=0.
\]
This is well defined under nonzero rescaling of representatives, and loops
are retained when a point is self-orthogonal.
\end{definition}
