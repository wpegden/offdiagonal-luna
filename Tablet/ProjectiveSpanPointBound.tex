\begin{helper}
Let $K$ be a finite field, let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $K$-vector space,
and let $S$ be a finite set of vectors.  The projective points contained in
the linear span of $S$ are at most $|K|^{|S|}$ in number:
\[
 \left|\mathbb P(\operatorname{span}_K S)\right|
 \leq |K|^{|S|}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
The span of $S$ is a finite-dimensional vector space.  Its dimension is at
most $|S|$, since the span is generated by the vectors in $S$.  A vector
space of dimension $r$ over $K$ has $|K|^r$ vectors, and its projective
points are represented by nonzero vectors, so their number is at most
$|K|^r$.  Since $r\leq |S|$ and $|K|\geq1$, this is at most
$|K|^{|S|}$.
\end{proof}
