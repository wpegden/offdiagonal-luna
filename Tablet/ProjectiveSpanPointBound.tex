\begin{helper}
Let $K$ be a finite field, let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $K$-vector space,
and let $S$ be a finite set of vectors.  Put
$W=\operatorname{span}_K S$ and $r=\dim_K W$.  Then the number of projective
points contained in $W$ is exactly
\[
 |\mathbb P(W)|=\frac{|K|^r-1}{|K|-1}.
\]
In particular, if $r=0$ then $\mathbb P(W)$ is empty, while if $r\geq1$
then
\[
 |\mathbb P(W)|\leq 2|K|^{r-1}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
The span $W$ is finite-dimensional, and a finite-dimensional vector space of
dimension $r$ over the finite field $K$ has $|K|^r$ vectors.  Each projective
point is a one-dimensional subspace, so its nonzero vectors form an orbit of
the multiplicative group $K^\times$, of size $|K|-1$.  The nonzero vectors
of $W$ therefore partition into projective points, giving
\[
 |\mathbb P(W)|=\frac{|K|^r-1}{|K|-1}.
\]
For $r=0$, the numerator is zero and the projective space is empty.  For
$r\geq1$, a finite field has $|K|\geq2$, hence
$|K|-1\geq |K|/2$; the displayed quotient is consequently at most
$2|K|^{r-1}$.
\end{proof}
