\begin{definition}
For positive integers $s$ and $k$, $r(s,k)$ is the least integer $n$ such
that every loopless finite graph with $n$ vertices contains either a clique of
size $s$ or an independent set of size $k$.  Formally, the tablet represents
the graph order by the cardinality of its finite carrier and takes the
infimum, in the natural numbers, of the set of orders satisfying this
property.  The graph relation is symmetric, and the loopless hypothesis is
explicitly imposed in the defining property.
\end{definition}
