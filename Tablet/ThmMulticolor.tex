\begin{theorem}
For every fixed integer $\ell\geq3$,
\[
 r(s;\ell)=\Omega\!\left(2^{(\ell-1)s/2}\right).
\]
That is, there are constants $c_\ell>0$ and $S_\ell$ such that the displayed
lower bound with factor $c_\ell$ holds for every $s\geq S_\ell$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $\ell\geq3$.  For large $s$, use
\noderef{F2ForwardIndependentBound} with $k=s$ to obtain the explicit
digraph $D=D_2(s)$, which is loopless and $T_s$-free, on
\[
 N=(1+o(1))2^{2s-3}
\]
vertices and
\[
 \operatorname{fwi}_s(D)\leq2^{3s^2/2-5s/2+o(s)}.
\]
Choose $n=2^{\lfloor(s/2-4)(\ell-1)\rfloor}$.  The floor changes $n$ by only
a factor depending on the fixed $\ell$.

For sufficiently large $s$,
\[
 \binom ns\left(\frac{\operatorname{fwi}_s(D)}{N^s}\right)^{\ell-1}<1:
\]
indeed, use $\binom ns\leq n^s$, the displayed tuple bound, and
$N\geq2^{2s-4}$; the base-$2$ exponent is at most
\[
 s\lfloor(s/2-4)(\ell-1)\rfloor+
 (-s^2/2+4s+o(s))(\ell-1)<0.
\]
The random-homomorphism lemma \noderef{RandomHomomorphismColoring} therefore
provides an $\ell$-coloring of $K_n$ with no monochromatic $K_s$.  By the
definition of \noderef{MulticolorRamseyNumber}, its Ramsey number is larger
than $n$.

Finally, for fixed $\ell$, the rounding and the constant $4(\ell-1)$ are
absorbed into a positive constant $c_\ell$, giving
$r(s;\ell)\geq c_\ell2^{(\ell-1)s/2}$ for every sufficiently large $s$.
\end{proof}
